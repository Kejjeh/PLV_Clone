======================================================================
METHODOLOGY NOTES
======================================================================
- SP identified as: pitcher with min inning = 1 AND >= 40 pitches in game.
- fp_proxy_per_bf = (K - BB - H - HR) / BF  (matches sp-breakout-signal calibration)
- whiff_pct = (swinging_strike + swinging_strike_blocked + foul_tip) / swings
- csw_pct = (swinging_strike variants + called_strike) / total_pitches
- xwoba_contact = sum(estimated_woba_using_speedangle WHERE launch_speed IS NOT NULL) / n_bip
- ev_mean = mean(launch_speed WHERE launch_speed IS NOT NULL)
- BABIP proxy = (H - HR) / (BF - K - BB - HR)
- Bootstrap: 10,000 resamplings of the signal-firing pool (with replacement)
- RoS = starts 7 through end of season (GS > 6)
