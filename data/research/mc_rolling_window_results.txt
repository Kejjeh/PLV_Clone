BASELINE DISCREPANCY NOTE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 2021-2025 rolling-window baseline (36.0%) differs from the prior calibration
(25.4%) documented in the sp-breakout-signal skill. Investigation shows:
  - Per-year good-start rates: 2018=33.3%, 2019=32.8%, 2020=33.7%, 2021=35.6%,
    2022=34.7%, 2023=32.8%, 2024=34.8%, 2025=34.5%.
  - The threshold -0.0476 IS the 65th percentile across 2018-2025, meaning ~35%
    of starts exceed it — consistent with this calibration, NOT the 25.4% figure.
  - The original 25.4% baseline was likely computed with a different formula,
    a different BF filter, or a different definition of "good start."
  - This re-calibration treats the formula fp_proxy = (K-BB-H-HR)/BF as canonical
    (matching the skill's own code at line 103 of SKILL.md).
  - RECOMMENDATION: Update the sp-breakout-signal skill's stated baseline from
    25.4% to 36.0% (2021-2025) or ~34.5% (all years 2018-2025).
  - The LIFT (delta) figures are the actionable signal; the absolute rates and
    tier labels should be re-anchored to the new baseline.
