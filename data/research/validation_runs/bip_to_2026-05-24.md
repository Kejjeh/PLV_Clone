### Why this failed

bip_to is mechanically near-collinear with `pa_to` (high BIP requires high PA), and `in_play_pct_to_sh × pa_to` already encodes the joint of rate × volume. The marginal "volume info" hypothesis is structurally absorbed by `pa_to` plus the cumulative shrunk rates. Same failure mode as `started_pct_to` from 2026-05-23.

### Decision

REJECTED. Do not add to RH3_FEATS. Permanently out — redundant with `pa_to` + the cumulative rate features. Holdout being 2/2 positive is interesting but pooled Δr and per-year sign fail the bar.
