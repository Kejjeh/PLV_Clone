Modern literature (Lichtman 2010s, Cameron) suggests SP performance is roughly flat at 4-6 days rest with mild degradation at <=3 (over-extension) and >=7 (rust / coming-off-IL). The "between-start rhythm" theory predicts a small negative coefficient on extra rest beyond the 4-day modal value. If a meaningful signal exists, expect partial r |~0.01-0.02|.

# Pre-registration body (written BEFORE running any models)

## Why this candidate
- RP3_FEATS encodes cumulative season state, IL stint structure, and within-season recency drift on rate metrics — but does NOT directly encode the inter-start rest pattern.
- Anecdotally, 6-man rotation moves, rainouts, and post-IL ramp-ups produce non-standard rest intervals that may carry RoS-relevant information about the pitcher's usage regime.
- This is a low-cost feature to compute (purely calendar-day arithmetic on already-loaded statcast pitcher game_date columns) so it is worth testing.

## Rule 8 framing
- Production use case is in-season → RoS at split rows of split_day = 30, 37, 44, 51, 58 (early-season decisions when waiver/drop calls actually happen).
- Convergence check: re-validate at split_day 30, 44, 58 (closest available match to the requested 30/42/56 — rolling_pitchers cache snaps to 7-day grid).
- All rest computations use ONLY game_dates strictly less than cutoff_date.

## Rule 9 baseline (the critical one)
- Baseline = the EXACT current RP3_FEATS from src/plv_clone/models/xfp/rp3.py, all 24 features. No stripping.
- The candidate `days_rest` is ADDED to that full baseline. Lift = cross_year_r(baseline + days_rest) − cross_year_r(baseline alone).

## Rule 5 sample-size honesty
- Per-year training cohorts: 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023 — 5 years × ~1300 rows per split_day × 3 split_days >> n=200 pooled floor.
- Holdout: 2024, 2025. Well above n=100 floor.
- Days_rest coverage: depends on the pitcher having ≥ 2 prior starts at the cutoff. At split_day=30 a portion of pitchers have only 0-1 prior starts; those rows get days_rest = 5 (population median, neutral).
- **Opener / bullpen-game handling:** any rest interval < 3 days is clamped to 3 (treated as standard short rest). Any rest interval > 7 days is clamped to 7 (treated as long rest, capturing the post-IL or extended-skip regime). The clamp prevents extreme outliers from dominating the linear regression coefficient.

## Rule 3 / Bonferroni
- This run tests TWO candidates (days_rest, lineup_handedness_match). Bonferroni-correct: alpha_per_test = 0.025 (from 0.05/2). Convert to the effect-size gate: still +0.005 cross-year r lift as the SHIP bar (the gate is on effect size, not on p-value).

## Decision rule (pre-committed)
- **SHIP** (verdict PASS): cross-year r lift ≥ +0.005 AND lifts at split_day 30/44/58 are all same-sign AND holdout 2024-2025 lift > 0 AND MAE reduction on holdout > 0.
- **DON'T SHIP** (verdict REJECTED): lift below +0.005 gate OR wrong sign on holdout OR lifts wildly inconsistent across split_days (leakage smell).
- **NEEDS MORE DATA** (verdict MARGINAL): lift in (0, +0.005) AND right sign on holdout AND no leakage smell — recommend wait for 2026 season completion before re-test.

## Leakage discipline pre-commitments
1. days_rest at row (pitcher P, year Y, cutoff CD) uses ONLY game_dates strictly less than CD.
2. Specifically: identify the SET of distinct game_dates for that pitcher in that year before CD. days_rest = max(distinct dates) − second_max(distinct dates), in calendar days.
3. Pitchers with fewer than 2 prior starts → days_rest = 5 (population median). This is the neutral default.
4. Clamp to [3, 7]: values <3 → 3 (handles openers / piggybacks), values >7 → 7 (handles long IL gaps).
5. Leave-one-year-out: when predicting year Y, train on the other 4 training years (Y excluded entirely).
6. Holdout (2024, 2025) is NEVER touched during training-year tuning.

## Known confounds
- Long rest in May / June is overwhelmingly driven by IL stints, which are already encoded in is_on_il_at_split + days_since_il_return_imp + il_stints_to. The clamp to 7 deliberately reduces double-counting with these features. If days_rest still picks up signal after the clamp, it's measuring rotation-skip / 6-man / rainout regimes, not IL.
- Short rest (3 days) is rare for SPs and often indicates a bullpen "opener follow" role, which has very different RoS performance than starter usage. Clamping to 3 floors these but does not exclude them — coverage of the days_rest = 3 bucket should be ~1-3% of rows.
