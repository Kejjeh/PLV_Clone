### Why this failed

`hr_per_pa_to_sh × pa_to` already encodes raw HR demonstration as joint rate × volume. Adding raw `hr_to` is structurally redundant with the rate×volume joint and introduces collinearity that slightly hurts ridge-fit stability. The 2019 -0.0006 cell is the visible cost of that collinearity. Same failure mode family as `bip_to` and `started_pct_to`.

### Decision

REJECTED. Do not add to RH3_FEATS. The holdout 0/2 alone is disqualifying — this would degrade exactly the years that matter most for current production. The "cumulative power demonstration" hypothesis is fully absorbed by the existing shrunk HR rate × PA volume.
