## Deeper battery (same session, each test pre-stated)

- **A) Threshold robustness:** class hold rises monotonically with the
  whiff-jump cut (+5pp: +0.35 n=31 · +6pp: +0.40 n=24 · +8pp: +0.60 n=13 ·
  +10pp: +0.85 n=4) — but every cell is small and...
- **B) ...the continuous dose-response is NULL:** Spearman rho = −0.067
  (wrong sign, n=121 cleared POS). Mechanism SIZE does not predict hold in
  general; the tail gradient in (A) is not load-bearing. This kills any
  temptation to build a whiff-delta pricing curve.
- **C) The held-window conditional — CLEARS:** among 36 cleared POS breaks
  with two disjoint 4-start forward windows, first-window holders (≥0.5 of
  gap, n=15) continued at **+0.87** mean / 73% P(≥0.5) vs +0.25 / 38% for
  misses (n=21). Welch t = +2.31, one-sided p = 0.014. Survivorship
  disclosed: 79 breaks lacked 8 forward starts; their partial hold (+0.29)
  is population-typical, so the exclusion does not manufacture the result.
  Lopez held his first forward window at 1.63 → the conditional applies.
- **D) Process persistence:** in the +8pp class, forward-window whiff cools
  ~30% of the spike in 11 of 12 cases (Lopez already 32.0 → 27.3) while
  retaining most of the gain over the pre baseline. Partial league
