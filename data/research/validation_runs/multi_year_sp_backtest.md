## Executive Summary

Across 5 historical seasons (2021-2025), evaluating **3,229** (snapshot, start) projection-vs-actual joins covering ~weekly cadence at split_days [44, 65, 86, 107, 128, 149, 170], the production rp3 v3 model would have predicted SP FP/start with pooled **MAE = 7.21 FP** vs a naive "always project league average (10 FP)" baseline of **MAE = 7.70 FP**. Lift over baseline: **+0.49 FP MAE (6.4%)**. Top-5 weekly streamer (rank ≥ 50) hit rate (actual ≥ 9 FP): **70.8%**. Boom rate (≥ 20 FP): **18.3%**. Bomb rate (< 0 FP): **7.5%**. σ calibration: **OVER-CONFIDENT (only 21.6% in p25-p75 vs 50% target)**.

**Honest skill verdict: TIER-DEPENDENT — strong at the top of the board, weak at the bottom. Ace tier (#1-10) lift = +2.16 FP MAE (23% of naive), SP2/3 (#11-30) lift = +1.05 (12%), streamer (#61+) lift = +0.15 (2%). Pooled lift of +0.49 FP (6.4%) understates the model's value on premium SPs and overstates it on streamers. The streamer tier is the operational pain point: a +0.15 FP MAE edge on ~9 FP outcomes is barely better than a coin flip vs league average.**

## σ calibration verdict

Across all 3,229 backtest starts, **21.6%** of actual per-start FP outcomes fell within the model's p25-p75 interquartile band (theoretical target = 50%).

**Verdict: OVER-CONFIDENT (only 21.6% in p25-p75 vs 50% target)**

The model is OVER-CONFIDENT: its sigma is too tight. Actual outcomes are more variable than the residual-CI table suggests. Consumers reading p25-p75 as "likely range" will be surprised more often than once-in-two starts.

## Honest takeaway

There are two ways to read this backtest and they tell different stories:

**MAE story (pessimistic):** The pooled MAE lift is only +0.49 FP (6.4%). That is genuinely marginal. Per-start outcomes are so variance-dominated (SD ~9.5 FP across the whole panel) that even a good model cannot squeeze the error band much below ~7 FP. If you judge rp3 purely on "how close was the point estimate to actual," be modest about its predictive faith.

**Selection story (positive):** When you let rp3 pick top-5 streamers each week, it hits at **71%** vs **58%** for the panel base rate — a **+12.7%** hit-rate edge. It bombs at **8%** vs **14%** base rate — a **+6.7%** bomb-avoidance edge. Per-tier, the model is meaningfully sharper on aces (#1-10 MAE lift +2.16) than on streamers (#61+ lift +0.15). Operationally, **rp3 is most trustworthy for ranking SPs against each other within a tier, less trustworthy as a point estimate of weekly FP.**

**Sigma is broken.** Only **22%** of actual outcomes fell inside the model's p25-p75 band vs the 50% theoretical target. Treat xfp_rp3_p25 / xfp_rp3_p75 as a rough "model's best-guess range," NOT as a true 50% interval. Real per-start variance is ~2.3x wider than the residual table suggests. Highest-priority fix: residual-CI buckets should be widened or replaced with empirical per-tier sigma from the per-start panel, not LOO RoS-avg residuals.

**What to believe:** when rp3 says "Soriano 12.13 FP" vs "Kelly 8.38 FP," believe the *ordering* — Soriano really is a different tier than Kelly with a 70% vs 51% hit rate gap. But do not believe the *level* to within +/-2 FP, and do not believe the p25-p75 band represents an honest 50% range.

## Methodology + caveats

- **Years evaluated:** [2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025]. 2020 excluded by rp3 design (COVID).
- **Snapshots:** split_days [44, 65, 86, 107, 128, 149, 170] (roughly weekly Apr→Sep).
- **Window:** each snapshot is joined to actual starts in the next 7 days.
- **Train framing:** leave-one-year-out — to backtest year Y, we train on [2018, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025] minus Y. This matches rp3.cross_year_eval, so no temporal leakage.
- **Eligibility filter:** require gs_to ≥ 2 so we are not extrapolating from pitchers with zero MLB data.
- **Naive baseline:** always project 10.0 FP/start (panel mean).
- **Per-start panel coverage:** 22,657 starts across 2021-2025. 2021 sample is comparable to 2022-2025 (~4,500 starts/year), so all years are usable. 2018/2019 NOT evaluated because per-start panel doesn't cover those years.
- **Known limitation:** the rp3 training target is `ros_fp_per_start` (rest-of-season AVG per-start FP for that pitcher from the snapshot), not "next-7-day FP/start." We hold rp3's framing constant and measure how well its RoS-avg projection ALSO predicts the next 7-day window. This is the actual operational question: when you add an SP for a week, you're betting on RoS skill manifesting NOW.
