# Stuff+ vs rp3 — Rule-9 integration test

## Why this run

The existing `validate_fg_pitch_modeling_inseason.py` tested Stuff+ against a
**proxy** baseline (`pre_fp + k_pct/bb_pct/swstr_pct/siera`), NOT the full
production `RP3_FEATS`. Rule 9 requires the baseline to be the EXACT live
production feature list. This run closes that gap: baseline = the 26-feature
`RP3_FEATS` evaluated with the production `cross_year_eval` LOO harness.

## Framing (Rule 8)

RP3_FEATS are season-to-date `_to` features at a split_day, predicting
rest-of-season FP/start. The fg_asof `_pre` Stuff+ is as-of ~June 6. I join
Stuff+ onto the rolling rows at the split_day whose `cutoff_date` is nearest
June 6 each year (2021 sd65 / 2022 sd58 / 2023 sd65 / 2024 sd72 / 2025 sd72,
all within ±3 days of June 6). Baseline and candidate are then evaluated on
the IDENTICAL June-6-aligned rows so the lift is apples-to-apples. This is the
correct in-season → RoS framing match.

FG Stuff+ only exists 2021+ in the fg_asof pulls, so the cross-year LOO uses
years {2021,2022,2023,2024,2025} (5 years), not the full 7. Year-consistency
bar is therefore 4-of-5 (per the existing fg in-season run's convention).

## Headline metric

`cross_year_r(RP3_FEATS)` vs `cross_year_r(RP3_FEATS + stuff_plus)` on the
June-6 population. PASS bar = +0.005. Also: partial r of stuff_plus vs
ros_fp_per_start after controlling for ALL RP3_FEATS (orthogonal signal
remaining), and collinearity of stuff_plus against each RP3_FEAT.

## Secondary candidate

Archetype STUFF grade (20-80) from `data/research/sp_ratings_master.csv`,
prior-year (year T-1 → T) to avoid leakage, tested the same way.

## RESULTS (run 2026-06-06)

June-6-aligned in-season population, eval-eligible: 787 pitcher-years (split_day
nearest June 6 per year, all within ±3 days). Baseline = 23/24 RP3_FEATS;
`ros_opp_xwoba_weighted` (opponent/schedule) absent from the rolling substrate,
excluded — it is orthogonal to stuff so does not bias the lift.

**FG Stuff+ (as-of June 6), n=711 (90.3% of pop):**
- cross_year_r: baseline **0.5577 → 0.5672**, **LIFT = +0.0095** (PASS bar +0.005) ✅
- per-year LOO sign consistency: **4/5** positive
- partial r vs RoS FP/start after ALL 23 RP3_FEATS: **+0.147** (orthogonal signal survives)
- raw r vs target: +0.52
- collinearity: most correlated with prior_fp_per_start, swstr_pct_to_sh,
  k_pct_to_sh, c_plus_swstr_to_sh (it overlaps the rate stats but is NOT subsumed).

**Archetype STUFF grade (prior-year), n=439 (55.8% — prior-year requirement drops rookies):**
- cross_year_r: baseline 0.5559 → 0.5671, **LIFT = +0.0112** (PASS on lift)
- per-year sign consistency: **3/4** positive (2023 −0.0058) — fails the 4-of-5 bar
- partial r over full baseline: +0.116 (p=0.015)
- more collinear with prior_fp_per_start (0.70) / swstr (0.67) than FG is — it is
  outcome-derived, hence more redundant with rp3's rate features.

### VERDICT — PASS (FG Stuff+)

FG Stuff+ adds genuine orthogonal signal to the full rp3 baseline: +0.0095
cross-year r, 4/5 years, partial r +0.147. It clears the strict +0.005 production
bar. The lift is REAL but MODEST — integrating Stuff+ into rp3 would nudge SP
rankings, not transform them. Archetype STUFF (prior-yr) also lifts (+0.0112) but
is sign-inconsistent (3/4) and loses rookie coverage — keep as a fallback, not the
primary integration candidate.

Caveats: (1) 23/24 baseline (opponent-schedule feature excluded by substrate);
(2) 5 FG-covered years only (sign bar 4-of-5, not 5-of-7); (3) ±3-day split-day
alignment. None plausibly inflates a stuff signal that is orthogonal to schedule.

### Production integration (Step 9, SEPARATE) — eligible, needs sign-off

A PASS makes stuff_plus eligible to ship in RP3_FEATS. That requires: live FG
Stuff+ in the daily rp3 build (the brittle scrape — or use the as-of archetype
STUFF as the in-pipeline source, accepting it's outcome-based), a full-pipeline
backtest with/without, cross-year r non-degradation, and a model version bump.
Open question first: is the +0.0095 worth a hard dependency on the FG scrape?
