# Velo / Times-Through-Order (TTO) decline-signal study

- **signal:** in-game velo fade (FB velo TTO3+ minus TTO1), 3rd-time-through wOBA/K% penalty, fade x season-velo interaction
- **outcome:** rest-of-season BrownU SP FP/start (`ros_fp_per_start`)
- **verdict:** see VERDICT section
- **date:** 2026-06-13
- **script:** `scripts/_oneoff/velo_tto_study.py`

## Methods

- **Panel:** `rolling_pitchers_2018_2026.csv`, years 2021-2025, split_days {51,72,93,114}, gated `gs_to>=5 & ros_gs>=3`. Joined feature rows: **2897**.
- **TTO definition:** native Statcast `n_thruorder_pitcher` (full coverage, true times-through-order, NOT the batter-order proxy). TTO1 = thruorder 1, TTO2 = 2, TTO3+ = >=3.
- **Leakage-safe AS-OF:** for each (pitcher, year, cutoff) all TTO features built ONLY from pitches with `game_date < cutoff_date` that season.
- **In-game fade** = mean FB (FF/SI/FC) `release_speed` in TTO3+ minus TTO1, gated >=30 FB pitches in TTO3+.
- **TTO penalty** = (wOBA TTO3+ minus TTO1) and (K%/PA TTO3+ minus TTO1), gated >=20 PA each bucket.
- **Baseline (Rule 9):** controls = whiff/K LEVEL `rank(swstr_pct_to)+rank(k_pct_to)` + `fp_per_start_to`. Second bar adds **overall velo YoY** (`avg_velo_to` minus prior-year season-end velo) — the real bar.

## VERDICT

**REJECT — do NOT wire any TTO / in-game-fade signal. Overall velo decline already dominates.**

1. **In-game velo fade (v3-v1) is a null with the WRONG sign.** Partial-r over the
   level baseline = **-0.0198**, and over [level, fp, velo_yoy] = **-0.0184** (n=1820).
   The bust-tercile table is the tell: the MOST-fading tercile has the *lowest* bust%
   (29.0%) and the *highest* mean RoS (10.75), not the worst. In-game stamina fade does
   not predict forward decline at this horizon. Hypothesis (a) FAILS.

2. **3rd-time-through wOBA penalty has a whiff of signal but loses to velo.** Partial-r
   over the level baseline = **+0.0625** (n=2595) — real but modest — and it DECAYS to
   **+0.0429** once overall velo YoY is controlled. It never approaches the velo bar
   (velo_yoy partial-r **+0.1198**). The K%-penalty is negligible (+0.030). Hypothesis
   (b) does NOT beat the existing flag.

3. **No fade x velo-decline interaction.** The "BOTH fade & losing velo" group busts
   **36.3%** — LESS than "losing velo only" (**46.0%**). The bust signal lives entirely
   in season-long velo loss (already validated as v2y/vYoY/vIn), not within-game fade.
   Interaction product term partial-r = **+0.018** (n.s.). Hypothesis (c) FAILS — fade
   does not amplify the velo-decline bust; the overall-velo lens already captures it.

4. `fb_velo_tto1` (a velo LEVEL, not a TTO construct) carries +0.118 — but that just
   restates the known velo-level signal, not a TTO finding.

**Bottom line:** TTO / in-game velo fade adds NO incremental decline-risk signal over the
already-validated overall FB-velo decline flags. The only TTO term with any partial-r
(wOBA-penalty +0.063) is non-additive once velo is controlled and well below the velo bar.
Clean, valuable NULL: keep the SP decline board on overall velo decline; do not add TTO
features. No thresholds to ship.
