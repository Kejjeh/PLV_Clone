xwoba: per-PA vs on-contact

xwoba_per_pa (r=+0.717) is the right metric to compare to Baseball Savant leaderboards. It divides by PA-ending events (woba_denom=1).

xwoba_on_contact (r=+0.513) measures quality of contact specifically on balls in play — useful for assessing approach on contact, but intentionally ignores strikeouts and walks.

The gap between them (0.717 vs 0.513) quantifies how much K/BB rate matters relative to pure contact quality.

Decision+ — Keep as Standalone

r = −0.168 with FP/PA means it was hurting the Process+ composite. Removed from the score.

However, Decision+ does correlate with walk rate (r ≈ +0.55) and is kept as a standalone column. In OBP-heavy formats (categories with BB or OBP), use it as a supplemental signal for patience/plate discipline. Do not use it as a fantasy production predictor in standard formats.

Updated Usage Guidance — How This Changes How You Work the Model

🎯 Pitcher Ranking — Use plv_blended

Raw PLV is still fine, but plv_blended is the primary ranking metric for pitchers going forward. At r=0.989 it explains 98% of FP/IP variance.

For veterans, blending prevents overreaction to hot starts and cold slumps. Watch the delta: a pitcher with plv_blended significantly above plv is being boosted by history — buy. Significantly below means history says the hot start is unsustainable — sell or wait.

📍 Hitter Ranking — Use Both process_plus and proc_plus_positional

process_plus for absolute production rank across all hitters.
proc_plus_positional for within-position scarcity value. This is what matters for roster construction — a proc+103 catcher can be more valuable than a proc+110 outfielder if catchers are thin.

Rule of thumb: if proc_plus_positional > process_plus by 5+ points, the player has above-average positional scarcity value on top of raw production.

💰 Closer Value — Add sv_hd_fp_per_162 Separately

The correct total pitcher value is: fp_per_ip × projected_IP + sv_hd_fp_per_162.

A closer with fp_per_ip=2.5 and 140 SV/HD FP is more valuable than a SP with fp_per_ip=3.0 and zero SV/HD FP, at typical reliever/starter IP splits. Do not compare fp_per_ip rates directly between SPs and RPs without adding the sv_hd contribution.

⚠️ blend_weight as a Confidence Gauge

Low blend_weight means the estimate is mostly based on prior-year data. For a hitter with 50 PA and blend_weight=0.14, the model is 86% prior history. The current-year signal matters little yet.

Use blend_weight as an explicit confidence gauge. Players with blend_weight < 0.20 and large divergence from their prior history deserve extra scrutiny before acting.

📊 K-Avoidance+ — Supplemental, Not Primary

Now correctly named. r=+0.363 is real and useful — it's the strongest component of Process+ relating to strikeout suppression. But don't lean on it as a standalone signal. It already has its full weight inside process_plus. Use it to understand why a Process+ score is where it is.

📋 Decision+ — OBP Formats Only

r=−0.168 in standard formats. Removed from composite. Only use Decision+ in OBP/walk-heavy scoring formats where BB itself generates points. In those formats, Decision+ (r≈+0.55 with walk rate) is a genuinely useful supplemental signal — filter for high Decision+ players when OBP is a scoring category.
